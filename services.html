Available Services

Telehealth Visits

Telehealth allows you to receive medical care from the comfort of your home through a secure video or phone visit. It’s a convenient way to discuss health concerns, manage medications, and receive guidance without traveling to the office.

We offer telehealth services across Florida for wellness care, preventive visits, and medical weight management. Simple, private, and accessible from your phone, tablet, or computer.

Weight Loss Management

Our weight loss management program focuses on helping you achieve healthy, sustainable results. We evaluate factors such as metabolism, hormones, lifestyle, and nutrition to create a personalized plan that supports your goals.

Treatment may include lifestyle guidance, medical support, and medications when appropriate, with ongoing monitoring to help you stay on track.

Aesthetic Services

Our aesthetic services focus on enhancing your natural beauty and supporting healthy skin. Treatments may include neurotoxins that help relax targeted facial muscles to soften the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles.

These treatments are minimally invasive and designed to provide a refreshed, natural-looking result while maintaining your unique features.

Corporate Wellness

Investing in employee wellness helps create a healthier, more productive workplace. Our corporate wellness services provide businesses with convenient health support designed to improve employee well-being, energy, and overall performance.

We offer services such as preventive health visits, wellness screenings, weight management support, and health education—delivered through on-site visits or telehealth options for convenience.

Supporting your team’s health can lead to better engagement, reduced absenteeism, and a stronger workplace culture. Ideal for companies looking to offer valuable health resources to their employees.

What Is Direct Primary Care (DPC)?

Direct Primary Care (DPC) is a healthcare model where patients pay a flat monthly fee directly to a primary care provider instead of using traditional insurance. This membership-based approach covers routine visits, preventive care, and some basic medical services without copays or deductibles.

Basic Primary Care Membership

  • $100 one-time enrollment fee
  • $95 monthly
  • Includes comprehensive primary care, extended visits, direct provider access, care coordination, and initial routine laboratory evaluation
