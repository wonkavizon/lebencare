About Us

Leben Care was founded with a mission to provide accessible, compassionate, and high‑quality healthcare to individuals and families across Florida. We believe healthcare should be personal, transparent, and tailored to each patient’s needs.

Our approach blends modern telehealth convenience with the warmth of in‑office care, ensuring every patient receives the attention and support they deserve.

Our Philosophy

We focus on building long‑term relationships with our patients, offering flexible telehealth services, in‑office care, and wellness programs designed to support your health journey.

Our Commitment

Whether you’re seeking routine care, chronic condition management, or convenient telehealth visits, our team is dedicated to delivering care that is both professional and compassionate.

Our Team

Karen is a dedicated and compassionate Family Nurse Practitioner committed to providing high‑quality, patient‑centered care. Her medical journey began in the Caribbean, where she completed her medical studies and developed a strong foundation in clinical medicine and community health.

After years of providing compassionate care in underserved areas, Karen continued her career in the United States, where she became a licensed Nurse Practitioner. She combines her global medical background with evidence‑based nursing practice to deliver comprehensive care that emphasizes education, prevention, and holistic well‑being.

Her clinical interests include primary care, chronic disease management, women’s health, and aesthetics. She is passionate about empowering patients through knowledge and compassion, ensuring they feel heard, supported, and cared for every step of the way.
